      How to read this

      Each dot is a driver. A link connects teammates whose qualifying or race performances were compared. Drivers who change teams connect those comparisons across the field.

      The horizontal axis shows the average year raced; the vertical axis shows overall driver strength at the driver's most recent appearance through the selected weekend. Select a driver or a teammate in the panel to explore their record.

      The vertical score is the published overall driver rating at each driver’s latest appearance. Its recent-era reference is 1,000: the median of the 20 highest-rated drivers at earlier weekends, with a 20-weekend half-life. The links show teammate evidence in the unified model, not a separate ranking. Historical ratings use later races; early estimates depend on the model’s starting assumptions.
