# Retrospective continuous-form component follow-up

Declared after the first fixed-family results were inspected. The original comparison and its failed selection gate remain unchanged in `results.json` and [the initial protocol](protocol.md). This is a post hoc follow-up, not independent confirmation.

The continuous driver-and-car form family reduced frozen-evidence teammate log-loss by 0.01851 against source `7a44023e0b230847`, while its pooled pre-qualifying combined log-loss improved by only 0.00033. Its four chronological blocks each improved at that ordinary metric, but it failed the predeclared requirement of at least 0.001 pooled improvement. Innovation-only and combined families also failed their original gates. No first-trial score or original acceptance result is relabeled as a pass.

For this follow-up, prioritize the user's frozen-evidence transfer prediction objective. Keep the continuous state, data, rating histories, fixed reference heads, observation weights, model design, chronological folds and the three-value ridge-strength grid unchanged. Separately refit driver-only, car-only and no-form heads; do not zero features in a previously fitted head. Compare them with the previously fitted continuous both-components head on exactly the same observations and same frozen evidence cutoff. The strict long-term frozen-rating benchmark remains unchanged; forecasts after joining are separate adaptive diagnostics.

A candidate is eligible when its pooled frozen-evidence move-balanced loss improves by at least 0.010 versus the archived reference, its pooled pre-qualifying combined loss does not worsen, and every other original pooled and per-block guard remains satisfied. Specifically, post-qualifying combined and qualifying pooled losses may worsen by at most 0.001, and pre-combined, post-combined and qualifying loss in each historical block may worsen by at most 0.003. These are point-estimate rules and do not prove equivalence. Select the lowest frozen loss among eligible masks, preferring a simpler mask only when both its pooled pre-combined and frozen losses are no worse than the both-components model.

Report every component, the unaltered first-trial failure, weighted log-loss and Brier, calibration, per-fold and paired season intervals, frozen-evidence move/season intervals, exact coverage and separately labeled adaptive mover forecasts. Every historical period has informed development. Fit the 2026 deployment head only using seasons through 2025, retain the existing grid head, and use 2026 for monitoring only. The selected follow-up is contingent on train/serve parity, source integrity and frozen-rating invariance; no deployment is authorized by this protocol.
