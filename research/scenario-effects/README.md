# Circuit and wet-weather scenario validation

The published model's circuit offsets learn from its own pre-weekend race prediction residuals. The separate wet-race calibration remains active, but driver-specific wet effects are disabled in production. The experimental wet learner remains available for this comparison only.

`node research/scenario-effects/evaluate.mjs research/scenario-effects/results.json` replays the published rating model chronologically. It compares fixed circuit-only and circuit-plus-wet rates with zero adjustments on the same weighted finishing-order pairs, using the scenario calibration. Each weekend's evidence updates the effects after its forecasts. In the interactive what-if, the final circuit offsets use the full available history even when a historical rating is selected; they are not a historical forecast. The comparison covers 2008–2025; the curated wet-race labels cover 2000–2025, with no established 2026 coverage. The extension uses the same historical wet-track source documented in `packages/core/src/wet-races.js`, including its classification conventions. Unlabelled races and partial wet conditions may be misclassified.

The circuit-only log-loss is 0.391995 versus 0.392506 without the adjustment, a difference of −0.000511 over 363 races (paired race-bootstrap 95% interval −0.000837 to −0.000166). Each of the four historical blocks improves slightly. Circuit-plus-wet loss is 0.391922, but the difference versus no effects has an interval of −0.001227 to +0.000046. On 15 labelled wet races in 2021–2025, circuit-plus-wet loss is 0.505629 versus 0.503531 without effects; its difference interval is −0.006609 to +0.010790. These intervals resample races but do not capture recurring drivers or model selection. Historical data informed earlier model development and the rates; none of these comparisons is independent confirmation.

The rates tested are inherited settings, not a newly optimised search. The recent wet slice and wider interval do not support a reliable driver-specific wet effect, so the installed wet rate is zero. The [results](results.json) contain the individual block scores, Brier scores, coverage and intervals.
