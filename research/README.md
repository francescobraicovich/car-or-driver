# Research evidence

The [unified model](../docs/unified-model.md) is the production baseline. Its original selection study is retained below; older filter, form and scenario experiments describe the previous model.

- [Current form](current-form/protocol.md): forecast-head design and retrospective selection.
- [Evaluation](evaluation/README.md): model evaluation results and historical evidence.
- [Benchmarks](benchmarks/README.md): comparisons with published research and their recorded results.
- [Joint-evidence graph](graph-evidence/README.md): teammate graph research baseline combining order and time margins, with separately validated extensions.
- [Unified ratings](unified-ratings/README.md): one driver/car model with qualifying, race-pace and overall outputs; selected production baseline, original validation and integration record.
- [Unified recent form and forecasts](unified-form/README.md): comparison of no form, external residual form and joint slow/fast states; retains no form and adds a validated conditional grid head.
- [Newcomer priors](newcomer-priors/README.md): relative driver/car entry priors; three bounded comparisons rejected on newcomer accuracy or stability, retaining production.
- [Scenario effects](scenario-effects/README.md): validation of circuit and wet-weather adjustments.
- [Stable ratings](stable-ratings/protocol.md), [temporal graph](temporal-graph/protocol.md), and [simplification](simplification/protocol.md): rating comparisons and their protocols.

The current production model is in `packages/core/`; research programs that regenerate site findings and evaluation results are indexed in [packages/research](../packages/research/README.md). Retired experiments are available in Git history.
